About Fegor Homes Ltd

Over a decade of helping Nigerians find, invest in, and grow wealth through premium real estate.

Our Story

Built on Trust, Driven by Results

Founded in 2013 and headquartered in Ajah, Lagos, Fegor Homes Ltd was established with a singular vision — to make premium real estate accessible, transparent, and rewarding for every Nigerian client.

Over the past decade, we have grown from a boutique property firm into one of Lagos's most trusted real estate companies, serving clients across Nigeria and the diaspora. Our team of seasoned property consultants brings together deep local market knowledge with international best practices.

We believe real estate is not just about buying a property — it is about building a legacy. Every transaction we handle is guided by integrity, expertise, and a genuine commitment to our clients' long-term success.

🎯
