1 Site Name

Fegor Homes Ltd

The name Fegor Homes Ltd represents a professional real estate company dedicated to helping clients find, buy, sell, and rent premium residential and commercial properties. The name is distinctive, brandable, and immediately communicates the company's core focus — providing quality homes and property services.

The word "Homes" grounds the brand in its purpose, while "Ltd" signals a trusted, formally established business entity. Together, the name builds credibility and authority in the competitive real estate market.

Optional Domain: fegorhomes.com

2 Site Purpose

The Fegor Homes Ltd website serves as a comprehensive digital platform for property discovery, real estate services, and client engagement. The site will provide:

  • A showcase of available properties for sale and rent, complete with detailed listings, photos, and pricing information.
  • Information about the company's real estate services, including property management, buying assistance, and investment consulting.
  • A contact and inquiry form allowing prospective buyers, sellers, and renters to reach the Fegor Homes team directly.
  • An "About Us" section that builds trust by highlighting the company's experience, mission, and team.
  • Helpful resources and guidance for first-time homebuyers and property investors navigating the Nigerian real estate market.

3 Scenarios

The following scenarios reflect questions from target audience users — prospective homebuyers, renters, and property investors visiting the Fegor Homes Ltd website:

  • "I am looking to buy my first home in Lagos — what properties does Fegor Homes currently have available, and what is the price range?"
  • "I own a property and want someone to manage it on my behalf. What property management services does Fegor Homes offer, and how do I get started?"
  • "I am interested in real estate investment but I don't know where to begin. Does Fegor Homes provide investment consulting or advisory services?"
  • "How do I schedule a property viewing, and what documents will I need to prepare if I decide to make an offer?"

4 Color Schema

The color palette is derived from the Fegor Homes Ltd brand logo and is extended with complementary tones to create a premium, trustworthy, and visually appealing real estate website.

Deep Crimson #6B1414 Primary Brand Color — Header background, section borders, headings, navigation active state
Warm Orange #F5831F Accent Color — Buttons, highlights, call-to-action elements, hover states, icons
Dark Burgundy #2C1A1A Navigation background, footer background, dark overlays on hero images
Warm Off-White #FAF7F2 Main page background — creates a warm, premium feel without harsh contrast
Cream Gold #FFF4E6 Alternating section backgrounds — adds warmth and separates content areas softly
Charcoal #2C2C2C Body text — readable, professional, softer than pure black
White #FFFFFF Card backgrounds, forms, content panels, logo background

5 Typography

Three carefully paired Google Fonts are selected to balance elegance, professionalism, and readability across all screen sizes:

Font 1 — Playfair Display

Used for: Main headings (h1, h2), hero text, property titles, and section titles. Conveys luxury, elegance, and authority.

Find Your Perfect Home with Fegor Homes Ltd.

Font 2 — Raleway

Used for: Sub-headings (h3), navigation links, labels, tags, and button text. Clean and modern with a premium feel.

Premium Properties • Expert Guidance • Trusted Service

Font 3 — Lato

Used for: Body text, property descriptions, form fields, footer text, and general paragraph content. Highly readable at all sizes.

Fegor Homes Ltd is dedicated to connecting clients with exceptional residential and commercial properties across Nigeria. Our experienced team provides end-to-end support from property search through to completion.
